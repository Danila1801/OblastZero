# Steam Store Page — Oblast Zero

Copy for the Steamworks store page. Written in the Oblast register (CLAUDE.md §9, bible §7): post-
administrative, not post-apocalyptic. Nothing here raises its voice.

Store copy is marketing, which means it is the one place where the voice has to survive contact with a
buyer who has never heard of the game. The rule applied throughout: **the register does the selling, the
copy never explains the register.** No line says "a bureaucratic dystopia." The forms say it.

## Full description (5000 char limit)

```
THE OBLAST DOES NOT RAISE ITS VOICE. THE OBLAST FILES A FORM.

Administrative District Zero was contaminated on a Tuesday. The response was
procedural: a cordon, a quota, a standing order, and a form for reporting
deviations. None of it was rescinded. Some of it is still being processed.

You are registered for demographic adjustment. You have a bunker, a roster, and
a filing deadline.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SIXTY SECONDS, THEN THE DOOR

Every expedition begins with a real-time scavenge, first-person, on a clock.
Sixty seconds from the moment you enter to the moment the emission is logged.
The site holds more than you can carry — that is the design, not an oversight.
Your operator's pack has a hard ceiling in kilograms, and the depot floor holds
roughly twice it.

So the phase is not "grab everything." It is one decision, made repeatedly under
a running clock: what stays.

Take the crate or the medical tin. Take the route past the contaminated pit or
the long way round. Take the second crew member or the weight of supplies that
would have fed the first. The clock does not care which you pick, and it does
not stop while you decide.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THEN THE PART THAT LASTS

What you carried in becomes the bunker's stores. From there the game is turn-
based and quiet. Days pass. Rations go down. Radiation goes up. Crew health,
sanity, fatigue and contamination each move on their own schedule, and none of
them move back on their own.

Deviations are registered daily and require a response. Over a thousand written
events, drawn from a corpus that tracks what you did, who you antagonised, and
what is still open on your file. Choices are permanent. Consequences arrive
later, by correspondence, usually when the stores are already low.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THREE BODIES, NONE OF THEM ON YOUR SIDE

  THE SCALE SOCIETY — administers the district. Will process your case. Reads
  your survival as compliance and your compliance as consent.

  THE CORDON — 14th Independent Regiment. Holds the perimeter. Considers the
  question of who is inside it settled.

  THE KAFEDRA — studies the contamination. Would like to study you. Is careful
  to describe this as an opportunity.

Standing with one is standing against the others. Four resolutions exist. Three
of them have a signature at the bottom. The fourth is what happens when the
correspondence simply stops arriving, because there is no longer an office to
send it.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PERMADEATH, WITH A PAPER TRAIL

A lost bunker is a closed file. The run does not carry over — but the record
does. Salvage tokens, awarded on the outcome, are spent at the supply office on
standing entitlements: a ration supplement, a reinforced pack frame, prior
correspondence with a faction that will remember it. Every registration starts
from where the last one left the paperwork.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FEATURES

  ·  Two phases per run: a 60-second first-person scavenge, then turn-based
     bunker survival
  ·  1000+ written events, over 700 items, a crew roster with traits that
     change what the Oblast lets you attempt
  ·  Four resolutions, each with its own ending and its own price
  ·  Hard carry-weight economy — the site always holds more than the pack
  ·  Persistent meta-progression across permadeath runs
  ·  Seed-reproducible runs
  ·  Full English and Russian localization
  ·  Gamepad supported throughout; every key rebindable

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
EARLY ACCESS

Oblast Zero enters Early Access content-complete on its core loop: both phases,
all four resolutions, the full event corpus, meta-progression, and both
languages are in and playable start to finish.

What Early Access is for: more sites, deeper anomaly and mutant behaviour, more
of the event corpus reachable per run, and the balance work that only real
players produce. The plan is public and updated. Filed, as it were.

Retain this record. It will not be issued again.
```

*(Approximately 3,300 characters — comfortably inside the 5,000 limit, with room for a release-date line.)*

## AI content disclosure — REQUIRED

Per CLAUDE.md §10 and Valve's two-tier policy:

- **Select: Pre-Generated.**
- **Do not select Live-Generated.** Nothing in the shipped build calls a model at runtime; the event system
  resolves authored data. If a live-LLM feature is ever added this must change *and* carry guardrails.

Disclosure text for the form:

```
Some of the art, audio, and narrative content in this game was created with the
assistance of generative AI tools during development. All generated content was
reviewed, edited, and integrated by a human developer. No AI generation occurs
at runtime; all content ships pre-generated with the build.
```

Development tooling (coding assistants, MCP tooling) is **exempt** and must not be listed — over-disclosing
tooling invites questions the policy does not ask.

## Trailer notes (for capture)

- **Open on the clock, not on a logo.** Two seconds of the countdown at 60, then a hand taking a crate.
  The premise is legible in four seconds without a word of copy.
- **Cut on the refusal.** The `OVER CAPACITY — NOT LOGGED` notice is the game's whole design in one frame.
- **Do not cut to music stings.** The register is flat. Let the ambient carry it and let the emission be
  the only loud thing in the trailer.
- **End on a form, not on a title card.** The closing line is already written: *Retain this record. It will
  not be issued again.*
